Why reading is important?

Reading is important because it helps us learn, think, and communicate more effectively. It introduces us to new ideas, information, cultures, and perspectives while improving vocabulary, grammar, and writing skills.Regular reading can also strengthen concentration, comprehension, imagination, and critical thinking. Whether we read books, articles, or stories, reading helps us understand the world better and supports success in school, work, and everyday life.
